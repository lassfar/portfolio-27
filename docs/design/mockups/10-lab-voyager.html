The beat

Entrance 3D / R3F Text Scroll Interaction Hand-off
Scroll

Continued scroll after the Earth gallery pulls the camera back — Earth shrinks and recedes — then flies out to Voyager drifting in the dark. Fully reversible on scroll-up.

3D

Voyager is a solid, lit 3D model — the big high-gain dish (hero shape) with its feed tripod, the ten-sided bus, the long thin magnetometer boom, a scan-platform of cameras, and three cylindrical RTGs. Deliberately the one built, human-made object among the particle-formed worlds. Slow idle tumble + drag-to-rotate (same interaction as the Saturn / Earth).

Text

Title “Half-finished thoughts” writes in (Great Vibes). Copy: “This is where I tinker — small experiments in motion, shaders, and code. More will drift into orbit soon.”

Interaction

The Golden Record glows and shows a label (reuses the pin-label overlay from P27-59). Click it → the experiment tiles open in the same overlay the Earth gallery uses; the first tile lazy-loads the parked worlds scene.

Hand-off

Voyager drifts onward, the background deepens and the first distant stars of Contact appear. The message it carries seeds the galaxy — “send it into orbit.”

Why it's mostly wiring

Reused from what's already built
  • Fly-to camera → CameraRig + a useVoyagerAnchor
  • Drag / idle spin → Earth / Saturn logic
  • Record label → pinScreen + PinLabels
  • Experiment overlay → EarthGallery portal + store
  • First tile → parked worlds scene (P27-47)
New, and the main risk
  • Solid Voyager model — glTF or built R3F geometry + a lit material (a new render style; breaks the particle vocabulary on purpose)
  • useVoyagerAnchor, useLabStore
  • Scroll re-timing — lengthen the master pin, re-scope fractions, add an Earth plateau, re-base Earth's full-view gates to an Earth window
  • Lazy-loaded experiment tiles (dynamic import)
  • Poly / texture budget on mobile (Voyager + Earth on screen during pull-back)

The through-line

The star exploded and its particles kept reforming — Saturn, the Sun, the Earth: worlds made of light. Voyager is the one thing that isn't star-stuff — a solid, human-made craft, the object the maker built and sent out. Its Golden Record holds the experiments and carries the message onward into the galaxy where you're finally invited to say hello. Worlds of light, and the one small thing we made to travel between them.
