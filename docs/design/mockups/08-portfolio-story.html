Aymane’s Portfolio — the story

A single continuous scroll-journey through space. One person, told as a cosmos: a star is born, explodes, and its particles reform into planets, an orbit, and finally a galaxy. This is the real, developed story — use it to build the prototype from scratch.

For Claude Design. Build a single-page, dark, scroll-driven portfolio for Aymane Lassfar, a senior frontend developer — introverted, a photographer and someone who’s drawn since childhood. The tone is emotional and personal, not a CV. Concept: “Living Canvas” — every section is a space object, and the whole page reads as one journey through the cosmos. Recreate the copy and the section arc below faithfully; the 3D can be approximated (particles, glows, gradients) where a full WebGL build isn’t practical. Prioritise: the story arc, the copy, the palette/type, and the signature scroll interactions.

One material, many forms. The star is Aymane at his most raw; it detonates and the same particles reform into his planet (his inner world), then into the Sun the photography worlds orbit, and finally a whole galaxy to connect across. How it starts & ends

Start: a black, silent screen. One star is born — Aymane’s star — and ignites with light. His name writes itself beneath it, and a quiet line invites: To wander.

The explosion: the star doesn’t die. It detonates at full screen, releases itself, and the same particles that built it drift inward and become something denser — his planet, his inner world. Same material, different form.

The Sun: the settled core that survived the explosion becomes the Sun at the centre of the photography section. It is him — not raw and burning, but calm and gravitational. Nine worlds he caught revolve around it.

End: you arrive at a whole galaxy — slowly turning — and send a message that flies off as a shooting star into the dark.

The arc: from a single point of raw light to a whole cosmos. From making things alone to connection across the dark. One material, many forms. That is the story.

Notes for building the prototype

• Structure: one long single page, deep space-black throughout, generous vertical rhythm; each section is full-height and scroll-driven.
• Motion: smooth (inertia) scroll; sections pin and animations scrub with scroll position (assemble on the way down, reverse on the way up). Great Vibes titles write in / colour in; body copy fades up.
• 3D approximation: where real WebGL isn’t practical, approximate the star / Saturn / Sun / galaxy with dense particles, radial-gradient glows, and blur — keep everything made of “light,” never hard shapes.
• Signature beats to keep: (1) star zoom → explosion → particles reform into Saturn; (2) About text gray→colour fill on scroll over the blurred planet; (3) Skills web drawing in; (4) photography worlds orbiting the Sun, zoom-in → dissolve to photo; (5) Contact “send it into orbit” shooting-star submit.
• Voice: quiet, honest, personal — use the exact copy above. Photography = landscapes. No traditional “projects” grid; the site itself + the Lab are the showcase.
